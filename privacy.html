Roman Heart Publisher – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

Kurzfassung (Deutsch): „Roman Heart Publisher“ ist eine private App von Roman Heart, die ausschließlich Inhalte auf seinen eigenen TikTok-Account veröffentlicht. Gespeichert werden nur Zugangs-/Refresh-Token, open_id und Anzeigename dieses Accounts auf einem privaten Server. Bilder werden nur kurz über Cloudinary bereitgestellt und nach dem Posten gelöscht. Keine Weitergabe oder Verkauf von Daten. DSGVO-Rechte gelten; Kontakt: Romanheart71@gmail.com.

1. Controller

Roman Heart, author and content creator, private individual, Germany. Contact: Romanheart71@gmail.com

2. Scope of the app

Roman Heart Publisher is a personal tool used only by Roman Heart to publish his own photos and videos to his own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public, and it does not access, collect or process data of any other TikTok user.

3. TikTok services used

  • TikTok Login Kit (OAuth 2.0) to authorise the app for the owner's account.
  • TikTok Content Posting API to upload and publish content.
  • Scopes requested:
  • user.info.basic – open_id, display name and avatar of the owner's account, used to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • video.upload – upload content as drafts to the owner's account.
  • video.publish – publish content directly to the owner's account.

4. Data stored

  • OAuth access token and refresh token
  • TikTok open_id and display name of the owner's account
  • Technical post status data (publish IDs, success/error status)

This data is stored on a private, access-restricted server controlled by the owner. Secrets are kept in protected configuration and are never published.

5. Purpose and legal basis

Data is used solely to publish the owner's content to his own TikTok account (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR – operating the owner's own service and legitimate interest).

6. Media hosting (Cloudinary)

Images are temporarily uploaded to the owner's own Cloudinary account (Cloudinary Ltd.) so TikTok can fetch them via URL. Each file is deleted after the post has been published. Cloudinary acts as a processor; transfers outside the EU rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. Standard Contractual Clauses).

7. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, shared with third parties or used for advertising or profiling. Data is only transmitted to TikTok (to publish) and Cloudinary (temporary hosting) as described above.

8. Retention

Tokens are kept only as long as the app is authorised. Access tokens expire after about 24 hours, refresh tokens after up to one year. When access is revoked or the app is no longer used, all stored tokens and account data are deleted within 30 days. Post status logs are kept for no longer than 12 months.

9. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions, or by contacting the owner.

10. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Contact Romanheart71@gmail.com.

11. Children

The app is not intended for and may not be used by anyone under 18.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures protect stored data, including restricted server access and encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above will change accordingly.
